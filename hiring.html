Join the AISI Lab

We are building an interdisciplinary team at the intersection of AI, immunology, cancer biology, and systems biology. We welcome researchers from diverse backgrounds worldwide who share our passion for science and human health.

Research Scientist

We welcome research scientists with strong training in computational biology, artificial intelligence, cancer biology, immunology, or related fields. Candidates are expected to lead research projects, develop independent scientific ideas, and contribute to the growth of the lab.

Postdoctoral Fellow

We are recruiting postdoctoral fellows interested in ambitious interdisciplinary research at the interface of AI, systems immunology, cancer biology, and translational medicine. Both computational and experimental researchers are welcome.

Research Assistant

We welcome research assistants with backgrounds in quantitative sciences, computational biology, bioinformatics, molecular biology, cancer biology, or immunology. Positions are suitable for candidates seeking intensive research experience before further graduate training.

Students

We welcome motivated Ph.D., Master's, and rotation students interested in computational biology, AI for science, systems immunology, and cancer research. Students from either quantitative or biomedical backgrounds are encouraged to apply.

What We Look For

Candidates with strong academic training and a solid foundation in at least one of the following areas are particularly encouraged to apply:

Quantitative Sciences

Mathematics, statistics, computer science, machine learning, or related fields.

Computational Biology

Bioinformatics, genomics, single-cell and multi-omics analysis, or systems biology.

Experimental Biology

Immunology, cancer biology, molecular and cellular biology, or related biomedical sciences.
