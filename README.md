# AISI Lab — Laboratory of Artificial Intelligence & Systems Immunology

Official website of the **AISI Lab**, led by **Dr. Tiangen Chang** at the **Shanghai Institute of Immunology, Shanghai Jiao Tong University School of Medicine**.

Our research integrates **artificial intelligence, computational biology, and systems immunology**, with a focus on precision cancer immunotherapy.
